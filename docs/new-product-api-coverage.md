# V12 local product API coverage

Read-only source/API snapshot, 2026-11-22. Scope: current product/ V12-derived source and product-backend/local_server.py composition. Local preview http://127.0.0.1:5186 and backend http://127.0.0.1:18381 both returned the NEW_COMPANY_NATIVE_CANDIDATE health response. GET /openapi.json on the backend advertised 94 distinct path templates. No credentials, company records or model outputs were requested. No model calls or mutations were made.

“Mounted” below means route presence in the running OpenAPI plus source installation, not proof every authorized operation works. Placeholder names {c}/{company}/{companyId} are equivalent for this comparison. Wildcard bot-directory paths require the actual directory dispatcher; they do not establish unrelated company routes. The orchestration wildcard is an explicit 503 configuration-required handler, not an executor.

Health at this snapshot: modelExecution=NOT_CONFIGURED and workerExecution=NOT_CONFIGURED. Fresh Boss/Pico/Pixel/team visible-state acceptance and key handoff are being handled by Prime/QA; they are not inferred from health or source here.

## Exact additional route factories for AgentX3

These are candidate reuse points, not an instruction to mount them without dependencies. Keep a single instance's state and authority everywhere. Source paths below are under dashboard/backend/ unless noted.

1. **Password update, small mount:** open_webui.routers.auths.router APIRoute with path /update/password, copied under /api/v1/auths by the same explicit copy/compile_path pattern already used in local_server. Keep get_current_user/password validation/session behavior. Do not mount signup/bootstrap/reset flows incidentally.
2. **Enterprise messaging extras:** enterprise_messaging.install_enterprise_messaging(app). Requires existing app.state.company_messaging and company_messaging_session (already present from native_mount.install). It alters the store with EnterpriseMessagingMixin and creates its own tables. Test human DM/member mutation/presence and cross-company rejection.
3. **Company Drive / Projects:** open_webui.company_drive.assembly.build_native_router(verified_user, db_path, blob_dir, ..., native_database=...). Supply ns.paths.authority for company scope, ns.paths.main for native conversation membership, instance-local blobs and explicit UploadPolicy. Its ChannelDrive contract is the frontend's starli.company-drive.v2. Uploads remain disabled unless configured; record-only project creation and actual website build must stay distinct.
4. **Panels:** wave_panels.native_install.install_panels(app, verified_user=..., db_path=..., authority_class=..., drive_class=..., blob_write=..., blob_read=..., drive_factory=..., sheets_class=...). Reuse ChannelDrive factory from the prior item. It advertises only supplied adapters and reports unavailable sheet/email types. wave_panels.sheets.build_router supplies sheet endpoints. Do not import wave_panels.workspace_app: it imports the entire old app and side effects. Email adapter requires a real bounded RPC/service, not a dummy.
5. **Company memory:** starli_memory.workspace_api.create_workspace_router(factory, verified_user=..., capture_fence=..., service_factory=..., mutation_guard=..., ...). starli_memory.workspace_mount.install_workspace_memory shows assembly with request_service_factory, company and registry paths, but also personal-bot/_v3 bindings. Prefer binding the direct factory to this product's native company runtime and tested Windows MemoryStoreFactory; explicitly review personal routes rather than importing mismatched personal identity data.
6. **Per-bot tools/preflight:** starli_company_tools.install(runtime, drive_factory=..., messaging=True, research=..., user_lookup=..., mcp=...), followed by starli_company_tools.router.make_router(runtime, native_session). Requires an appropriate runtime.company_tools and scoped optional services. The factory intentionally marks personal/admin-only executors unavailable. Current local app exposes local_model_runtime, not automatically the app.state.company_bot_runtime expected by many production installers. Bind the local runtime explicitly; do not manufacture enrollment or model turns to populate a tool picker.
7. **Account MCP:** open_webui.starli_account_mcp.account_mcp.bootstrap.install(app, service=..., adapter=..., authenticate=..., capture_fence=...) and reviewed oauth_connect/oauth_development/oauth_external installers. Private registry storage helper is reusable; transport readiness is separate. Scope account-owned MCP into a company turn only through the canonical consent/delegation adapter.
8. **Customer API:** open_webui.routers.customer_http.make_routers(store, native, session_owner) is the lower-level contract; starli_customer_api.install(app) is the feature-flag wrapper. Supply CompanyAdapter/JoinedAdapter tied to the local company runtime and fresh authority. Its current chmod-only private state creation needs the shared starli_private_fs Windows policy before enabling on Windows. Do not grant access to personal bots by pretending the old personal store is the new product's authority.
9. **Broader providers:** starli_company_computer.runtime.company_bot_runtime.company_provider_api.create_router(store, verified_user) and company_provider_install.mount_company_providers require the complete canonical company provider store/SDK capability interface, not just a router rename. company_provider_images.install(app, runtime) adds capabilities/endpoints/interactive logins and image routes but requires its provider image/subscription runtimes and vault. Do not mount a second conflicting basic provider router over local_provider.install.
10. **External services, config/worker gated:** starli_engine_connections.native.install(app,runtime,service,allowed_origins=...); smtp_http.install(...); starli_company_social.http.make_router(service,native_session,fence_key); starli_automations.production.install_production_automations; starli_agent_triggers.production.install_production. Their production wrappers require real engine/vault/broker configs. Company browser uses enterprise_browser.native.attach(app,runtime,private_directory=...,enrolled_companies=...,native_session=...,boundary=...). Published-process list uses starli_company_computer.app_previews.install(app,runtime), requiring a real compute service; chat_vision.install requires an image-capable runner. These are not safe “just add routes” fixes.

The direct factory signatures were read in source; they were not invoked in this coverage task. A source-present factory can still need adaptation to the current local runtime. AgentX3 owns mounting and tests.
